# TWENTY-FOUR — Series Bible

Original endurance-racing drama for TikTok, Reels and Shorts (9:16, 90 s episodes). The visual language follows the supplied reference frames (ground-level dust shots, pit-wall close-ups, broadcast booth, mirror reveals, helmet visor close-ups, the podium press scrum, name titles, the hard cut to a dark night location, the rain-soaked face-to-face confrontation); the characters, cars, team, circuit and story are original.

## Premise

Teo Marsh, five-time winner of the Kestrel Bay 24 Hours, leads his final race by seconds with one minute left. His tyres are gone and his radio has gone silent. Closing in is Isla Ferreira, the reserve driver he kept on the bench for three years. The secret: Teo is losing the sight in one eye, and for the last six hours he has been driving from memory. His wife Mara is the only person in the crowd who isn't cheering. And the team has known far longer than anyone will admit.
