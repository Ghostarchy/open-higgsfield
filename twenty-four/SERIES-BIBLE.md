# TWENTY-FOUR — Series Bible and Episode 1

Original endurance-racing drama for TikTok, Reels and Shorts (9:16, 90 s episodes). The visual language follows the supplied reference frames (ground-level dust shots, pit-wall close-ups, broadcast booth, mirror reveals, helmet visor close-ups, italic centred subtitles); the characters, cars, team, circuit and story are original.

## Premise

Teo Marsh, five-time winner of the Kestrel Bay 24 Hours, leads his final race by seconds with one minute left. His tyres are gone and his radio has gone silent. Closing in is Isla Ferreira, the reserve driver he kept on the bench for three years. The secret nobody in his team knows: Teo is losing the sight in one eye, and for the last six hours he has been driving from memory.

His wife Mara is the only person in the crowd who isn't cheering. Series engine: every episode is one stretch of a single 24-hour race, told out of order, each ending on a revelation that changes what the last episode meant.
