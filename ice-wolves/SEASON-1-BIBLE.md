# The Blind Girl the Ice Wolves Chose — Season 1 Bible

Original world, characters and mythology. Structure only (tiny child, giant creature, terrified warriors,
one power moment, cliffhanger) follows the genre; no names, dialogue or storylines are borrowed.

---

## 1. The emotional engine (read this first)

Every episode runs on one contrast: **the smallest, most fragile person in the frame is the only one the
largest, most dangerous thing in the frame will not hurt.** Everything else serves that image.

Three rules that make it land:

1. **Mira never speaks in Season 1.** She is blind and four years old. She listens, touches, hesitates,
   reaches. Her first word is the season finale. (This is also a production rule: ElevenLabs blocks
   child voice design, and a silent child needs no lip sync.)
2. **The adults carry the words.** Asha's fear, Sigrun's jealousy and Grima's dread tell the audience how to
   feel. Cut to a warrior's face after every creature beat.
3. **Slow down before every touch.** Hesitation is where the emotion lives: the paw that stops, the hand
   that hovers, the breath that fogs her hair. Never cut on the touch itself; hold 2 seconds after it.

Recurring emotional devices:

- **The wooden wolf.** Mira's only toy, carved with a V-notch in its left ear. Vorr has the same notch.
  Nobody knows who carved it. It is the series' mystery object.
- **The lullaby.** A five-note melody Asha hums to Mira (instrumental only: solo nyckelharpa or cello). It
  plays under every moment of tenderness and nowhere else, so the audience learns to feel it.
- **Asha's hands.** She holds Mira's face in both hands whenever she is afraid for her. Repeat it every
  episode; it becomes a signature.

## 2. World

**The Hrimvald** — an all-female warrior clan in the frozen northern mountains. Every girl faces the
**Trial of the Pack** at seven. The ancient wolf **Vorr** has chosen no rider in a hundred years.

**The Frost Circle** — their ceremonial arena, carved from grey stone into a mountainside beneath a
glacier.

**The Black Gate of Skarn** — a colossal iron gate, chained shut, high on the pass above the clan. The
Hrimvald were founded to guard it. Nobody alive has seen it open.

## 4. Episode 1 — "The Smallest One"

**Logline:** Every girl of the clan fails the Trial of the Pack, including the chieftain's proud daughter.
Then Mira, a blind four-year-old foundling nobody thought to stop, follows the sound of the wolf's
breathing into the arena — and the beast that terrifies everyone curls around her like a mother.

**Twist (final 20 s):** the clan seer sees Mira's toy. Its carved ear has the same torn notch as Vorr's —
a scar Vorr took *the night Mira was found in the snow*. War horns. The Black Gate cracks open.

**Cliffhanger:** Mira, who has never pointed at anything, turns and points straight at the gate as her
eyes ignite ice-blue. Card: SHE WASN'T FOUND. / SHE WAS HIDDEN.

## 5. Season 1 arc (30 episodes)

Every episode: hook in 3–5 s, the big creature or character interaction at ~45 s, a twist in the final 20 s,
a cliffhanger card in the last 3 s.

**Act 1 — The Bond (EP1–10)**
1. The Smallest One — Vorr chooses Mira; the Black Gate cracks.
2. Something from the gate crosses the glacier at night; Vorr refuses to leave Mira's side.
3. Sigrun tries to tame Vorr in secret to prove herself; Mira finds her hurt in the snow.
4. Grima reveals the night Mira was found: a burning sky, a wounded wolf, a baby in a ring of melted snow.
5. The first creature from the gate — a pale stag the size of a tower with eyes on its antlers — walks into the village.
6. The stag bows to Mira too. The clan splits: is she a blessing or the reason the gate opened?
7. Asha is challenged for the chieftainship by the war leader who wants Mira cast out.
8. Mira wanders onto the frozen lake; something enormous moves beneath the ice.
9. A great sea-serpent breaks the ice; Vorr fights it; Mira's touch calms it.
10. The serpent leaves a gift on the shore: a second carved toy, identical, older, with a name burned into it.

**Act 2 — The Name (EP11–20)**
11. The name belongs to a queen of a fallen southern kingdom.
12. Riders arrive from the south claiming Mira as their lost princess.
13. Sigrun, jealous for ten episodes, is the one who saves Mira from being taken.
14. A dragon of black glass circles the Frost Circle; every warrior draws a bow; Mira waves.
15. The dragon lands and lowers its head to Asha, not Mira. Asha has a past.
16. Asha confesses: she was the queen's shield-sister, and she carried Mira out of the burning south.
17. The war leader seizes power while Asha is shamed; Mira is locked away.
18. Vorr tears down the gates of the hall to reach her.
19. The clan rallies behind Sigrun, who now speaks for her sister.
20. The Black Gate opens fully. What steps out has the face of Mira's mother, carved in stone.

**Act 3 — The Gate (EP21–30)**
21. The stone giant speaks in her mother's voice (an adult voice) and calls Mira home.
22. Every creature in the mountains gathers at the Frost Circle — on Mira's side.
23. Grima's prophecy: the gate can only close if the child walks through it.
24. Asha refuses. Sigrun volunteers to go in her place.
25. Sigrun walks into the gate. Mira follows her.
26. Inside the gate: a frozen city of giants, each holding a wooden toy.
27. Mira's eyes show her what she has been listening to her whole life.
28. The giants kneel. Sigrun understands why Vorr chose her sister.
29. The gate begins to close with them inside. Vorr holds it open with its body.
30. Mira speaks her first word. (Card: SEASON 2.)

## 6. Production rules (learned building EP01)

- **Start frames first.** Kling image-to-video uses its image as the first frame, so every shot needs its
  own start frame composed for that shot, built from the locked references. Animating a character portrait
  directly makes every shot open on the same pose.
- **Check every start frame before animating.** In EP01 v1, war paint leaked from Asha onto Mira twice.
  Keep the "only Asha wears war paint" instruction in any shot with both of them.
- **Lip sync = Higgsfield Speak** (`higgsfield-ai/speak`): image + shot-timed WAV, durations 5/10/15 s,
  outputs 704×1280 at 16 fps. Use it only for the seconds a mouth is visible on a line; cut back to the
  Kling clip for the action around it (as in EP01 v1 shot 11).
- **Speak loses scene action.** Never ask Speak for big movement; give it the face, keep the movement in
  Kling.
- **Text in the frame is unreliable.** Titles and cards are added in post (Cinzel), never generated.
- **Mira has no voice.** Do not design or clone a child voice. Her sounds are breaths and footsteps only.
- **Prompt length.** Every prompt above is under 3,000 characters with all locks repeated. If a model
  truncates, drop the setting block before any character block.
